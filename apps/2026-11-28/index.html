How it works

  1. Bid. Everyone divides the full rent across the rooms by how much they want each one.
  2. Assign. The Hungarian algorithm gives each room to someone so the total of winning bids is as high as possible.
  3. Price. "Nobody envies anyone" becomes a set of difference constraints, which Bellman-Ford shortest paths solve.
  4. Balance. Every price drops by the same amount until they add up to the rent, which gives the least happy person as much surplus as possible.
